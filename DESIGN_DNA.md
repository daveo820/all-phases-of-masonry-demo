# Design DNA: All Phases Of Masonry

- **Hero archetype:** running bond job wall. Serif headline on the left; on the right, a wall laid in running bond where every brick is a real job from an Angi review, with its price and month. Odd courses are offset with half bricks. Bricks lay in one by one on load. New in the portfolio.
- **Signature motif:** the course. Two rows of brick with mortar lines, the top row offset by half a brick, revealed left to right like a course being laid. Used at the bottom of the hero, under every subpage header, under the services band, and on top of the footer (4 to 5 per page), plus the logo mark and favicon (three bricks).
- **Motion:** fade plus 20px rise on `.rv`; course clip reveal; bricks drop 14px into place with a stagger. No blur. Reduced motion shows everything.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display, roman and italic; the italic carries the brick red accent lines) plus Mulish (body, 18px, line height 1.66). Self-hosted woff2 latin subsets, preloaded.

- **Placeholders:** moss tag "PLACEHOLDER" on a hatched brick pattern box.
